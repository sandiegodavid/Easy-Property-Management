# Operator experience: current interface conventions

## Purpose

Help the operator recognize records, act deliberately and distinguish known information from missing/unavailable information. Keep current facts, incomplete input, source evidence and proposed AI output visibly distinct.

## Navigation and recognition

Default destinations are Home, Properties, Owners, Leasing, Money, Maintenance, Providers and Settings. Operators can hide/reorder customizable destinations and choose Light/Dark. Home/Settings remain available; hidden destinations stay reachable under More and context. Property/Owner directories support multiple records, then contextual workspaces with explicit child sections.

Directories return source-backed labels, totals and bounded pages. Property/Owner views preserve current/former ownership, source links and independent section availability. Owner money is explicitly dated whole-property activity, not owner entitlement.

## Disclosure, editing and recovery

Click the entire item summary bar to expand or collapse details. The bar has no editing fields or separate action controls. Support keyboard activation and announce expanded state. Place forms/actions below it, preserve input through collapse and disclose when input is saved versus unsaved.

Consequential actions use explicit source review/confirmation where required. Preserve edits after conflict, retain original keys after lost responses and distinguish incomplete saved forms from committed domain drafts. File-reselection guidance must identify the expected evidence without storing bytes.

## Contextual domain workflows

Portfolio setup separates Property inventory, Space status and ownership. Shared Party/contact, Tenant and Provider setup reuse identity and show duplicate/lifecycle guards. Lease and Inspection workspaces preserve contractual versus physical dates, independent revisions and evidence membership. Missing inspection evidence prompts review without blocking truthful Lease lifecycle.

Money, repairs, communications, waiting, search/coverage and optional AI have approved remaining interaction requirements in [the active operator design](../active/UI-001_DESIGN.md). Home's final Needs action/Waiting/Upcoming/calendar composition is separate delivery. Empty queues must never imply complete property coverage, and no missing source is rendered as a reassuring zero.
