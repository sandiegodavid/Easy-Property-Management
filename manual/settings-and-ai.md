# Settings and AI

[Manual contents](README.md)

## Appearance and navigation

Open **Settings → Appearance and navigation**. Select Light or Dark. Choose which destinations appear and use Up/Down to reorder; Home/Settings stay fixed. **Restore defaults** resets the editable choices. Choose **Save preferences** to persist them; immediate appearance preview alone is not a confirmed save.

Hidden destinations remain under More and contextual links. Hiding Owners can simplify a self-owned portfolio without deleting owners/relationships or suppressing obligations. If preferences changed in another window, **Load current preferences**, review your retained choices and save deliberately.

**Provider categories** manages the provider catalog, not Maintenance or expense categories. **Unfinished forms** in the header opens saved-input/recovery; see [daily operation](daily-operation.md#save-resume-and-correct).

Workspace backup/location administration uses installer commands; there is no Workspace Settings page.

## Built-in AI availability

Open **Settings → AI assistance**. Settings supports Off, On this device and Cloud service configuration. Choices come from qualified registrations. The current production installation has no registered production adapters/actions/approval handlers; no model is downloaded by entering a label or key. Manual workflows remain available without AI.

When a release supplies a registered capability:

1. Choose a qualified adapter/model and the appropriate device/cloud connection.
2. For cloud use, review and explicitly save the required data-class disclosure for that named destination.
3. Enter secrets only in the write-only credential field. They go to the device credential store and are excluded from workspace backups.
4. Run the supported synthetic connection probe; inspect its result. A successful test is not proof every future source/action is permitted.
5. Enable/configure only permitted actions and limits; review redaction-profile summaries.
6. Use global pause to stop governed assistance when needed. Disable or change a connection through its specific controls.

A model key is not permission for an external personal assistant. Connected-assistant setup/transport is separately deferred. No fallback to a different cloud/device provider is implied.

## Review supported AI drafts

If a registered action and owning approval handler are available, open the draft review from the supported source/AI interface. Inspect frozen provenance, governed input and original-source comparison. Edit proposed output, save the edits and review the acknowledged saved version; editing clears prior confirmation. Approve only with current available source and an enabled owning handler. Dismiss/rework only through decisions actually offered by that action.

AI output is proposed/advisory. It does not send messages, sign agreements, contact providers or commit money without the supported explicit review action. Missing/stale/disabled capability explains why approval is unavailable; do not bypass it by making a second connection.

## Uncertain credential/probe outcomes

External-effect operations can have uncertain results. Follow original-key lookup and explicit acknowledgment before attempting another change. Never put credentials into notes, general unfinished forms, source content, screenshots shared for support or application configuration. After restore, credentials/connections may require fresh device authorization.

## Dashboard scheduling

Under **Settings → Home**, choose **Automatic refresh**: every 1, 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes. The default is **5 minutes**. This browser preference saves automatically and survives reloads; it is not included in workspace backups. Home refreshes its cards while visible, and Needs action may update sooner when a deadline or follow-up becomes due. Use **Refresh** to check immediately.

Home uses live reads, not a snapshot cache or a Dashboard cron job. Background scheduling independent of an open browser remains deferred.
