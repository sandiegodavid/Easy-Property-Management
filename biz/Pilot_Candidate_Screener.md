# Pilot Candidate Screener (5 Questions)

Use this on a prospect's first reply or a short call (3–5 minutes) to decide whether they belong in your 30-minute interview. Log the answers in the **Pilot Candidate Tracker** (SCREENER columns). The tracker's *Screener result* fills in automatically.

**Goal:** find independent managers (about 10–50 doors) who manage at least some rentals for other owners and will give you 30 minutes.

**Rules of the road**
- Don't pitch. You're researching how they work.
- Don't promise features or dates.
- Don't collect sensitive details: no tenant names, bank details, or ID numbers.
- Take notes; ask before recording.

---

## Opening (about 30 seconds)

> "Thanks for getting back to me. I'm building a simple tool for independent property managers and want to hear how people run things today. Before I book time with you, can I ask five quick questions to make sure I'm talking to the right people? It takes about three minutes, and there's no sales pitch."

---

## The five questions

**1. Doors managed** → *Doors managed*
> "Roughly how many rentals do you manage right now, counting each door or unit?"

Probes (optional): single-family vs. multi-unit? Growing or shrinking?

**2. Managing for others** → *Manages for other owners?* (Yes / Some / No)
> "Are any of those for other owners, or are they all yours?"

Probes: How many owners? Do you collect the rent, or do owners collect it themselves?

**3. Current tools** → *Current tools*
> "What do you use today to keep track of it all: spreadsheets, QuickBooks, a property management program, something else?"

Probes: Have you tried anything and dropped it? Why? (This feeds interview question 11.)

**4. Bookkeeping** → *Books handled by* (Self / Bookkeeper / Accountant / Staff / Other)
> "Who keeps your books: you, a bookkeeper, or an accountant?"

**5. Willingness** → *Willing to do 30 min?* (Yes / Unsure / No)
> "Would you be open to a 30-minute call where I ask how you run things and get your reaction to an early design? I'll send a thank-you for your time."

---

## Scoring (matches the tracker)

| Result | Rule (defaults) |
|---|---|
| **Qualified** | 10–50 doors, manages at least some for other owners, and says yes to 30 minutes |
| **Maybe** | 5–100 doors but outside the qualified range, or unsure about 30 minutes |
| **Not a fit** | Manages only their own, says no, or fewer than 5 or more than 100 doors |
| **Incomplete** | Fewer than three of doors, managing for others, and willingness are answered |

The 10–50 range comes from the interview plan. The wider Maybe band (5–100 doors) is my assumption **[hypothesis]**. Change the thresholds on the tracker's Rules sheet.

**Then:**
- **Qualified:** book the interview now (see below).
- **Maybe:** book one if you still need volume or variety (for example, a person who never used property software); otherwise thank them and keep the note.
- **Not a fit:** thank them, ask for a referral, and set Status to *Not a fit*.

---

## Booking the interview

> "Great. Would [day/time] or [day/time] work? It's a 30-minute video or phone call. I'll share an early design on screen, take notes, and send [thank-you] afterward. Is it okay if I record it, or would you prefer I just take notes?"

Note their recording preference on the tracker, and only record with a clear yes.

---

## Polite close for anyone who isn't a fit

> "Thanks so much for your time. This helps me a lot. You don't sound like the right match for this round, but I appreciate it."

## Referral ask (use with everyone)

> "Who else do you know who manages rentals for other owners and might be open to a short chat?"

Log the count in *Referrals given* and add each named person as a new row with Source set to *Referral: interviewee*.

---

## Reminders

- Screen out gently. A "no" is useful data, so log it.
- Keep outreach one-to-one and honest about what you're doing. Bulk cold texting, calling, or email can trigger rules like TCPA and CAN-SPAM. This isn't legal advice, so check with a professional if you scale up.
- The tracker holds personal information. Keep it in a protected folder and delete records if someone asks.
